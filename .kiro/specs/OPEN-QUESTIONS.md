## Measurement session (tasks 1 to 6 and 9)

- Tasks 7, 8 and 10 stay unticked. Tasks 7 and 8 are REAL BROWSER (DEVICE MODEL) and were not run. Task 10 needs their results recorded. `results/device-model-check.json` and `results/cold-start.json` do not exist, and the other H-files named in measurement R6.1 do not exist either. `npm run measure:order` lists them. - The exact pass caught every listed name in the set (all of the listed-name identifiers; the counts are in `results/catch-rate.json`). That is a property of the set, not a finding about real notes: the builder writes names the way the exact pass is designed to read them (whole names, parts, nicknames on the list, other case, a name split across a line). It does not write initials, names with several letters wrong, other alphabets, or names that are not on the list in any form. The figure is a ceiling on this set. The limits line on the page and in the README says the figures do not predict real use. The product owner may want harder notes before the figure is quoted anywhere.
- How identifiers are recorded (deviation from "every character of the span must be masked"). A name split across a line, and a hyphenated surname used alone, are recorded as one identifier per name part, because the line break and the hyphen name nobody and the exact pass works on name parts there. Whole names are one identifier. The scoring rule itself is unchanged: every character of an identifier must be inside a masked span.
